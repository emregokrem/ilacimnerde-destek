# İlacım Nerde? Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the İlacım Nerde? mobile application (the "App") handles data. The App
is developed by Emre Gökrem (the "Developer"). The App is not an official application of the
Turkish Ministry of Health or the Pharmaceutical Track and Trace System (İTS).

**In short:** The App has no accounts, sends no personal data to the Developer, and contains no
ads or analytics/tracking. The medicine and province/district you choose for a pharmacy search
are sent only to the Ministry of Health's public İTS website (its.gov.tr).

## 1. Pharmacy search (its.gov.tr)

To show which pharmacies stock a medicine, the App opens the Ministry of Health's public
"İlacım Nerede" page (its.gov.tr) in an in-app browser view and runs the search through that
page. The following is sent to its.gov.tr:

- The barcode (GTIN) of the medicine you selected
- The province and district you selected

This information is not linked to your identity. As with any internet request, its.gov.tr can
see your device's IP address and standard connection information and may use its own cookies.
This processing is governed by the Ministry of Health's own policies.

## 2. Verification (Google reCAPTCHA)

its.gov.tr requires a Google reCAPTCHA "I'm not a robot" check before searches. You complete the
check yourself; the App does not bypass or solve it automatically. reCAPTCHA sends device and
browser information (e.g. cookies, screen and browser properties, interactions) to Google to
tell humans from bots. This is governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

## 3. Camera

The App uses the camera to read the DataMatrix code or barcode on a medicine box so you can
select the medicine. Images are processed in real time on the device by the operating system's
barcode recognition components (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android). **Images are not
stored and are never sent to any server.**

On Android, Google ML Kit may send anonymous performance and usage metrics (e.g. device model,
OS version, processing time) to Google. These metrics do not contain camera images or the
content of scanned codes. See [Google ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

## 4. Location

The App does **not** use your device's location. You choose the province and district yourself.

## 5. Medicine and equivalent lists

To show medicine names and equivalent medicines, the App checks the current medicine price list
and the SGK equivalent medicine list at launch and downloads them when needed. The lists are
downloaded from the Developer's file storage on Microsoft Azure. No personal information is sent
in these requests; however, as with any internet request, the server can see your device's IP
address and standard connection information. The Developer does not use this to identify users.

## 6. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored **only on your device**, in the App's private storage, and is not
sent to the Developer or any third party:

- Your favorite medicines
- Your search history (medicine, province/district and date; at most the last 50 searches)
- App settings (theme, etc.)
- A copy of the downloaded medicine and equivalent lists
- Cookies of its.gov.tr and Google reCAPTCHA in the in-app browser view

You can clear the history from the Settings screen, or delete all data by uninstalling the App.

## 7. Calls, maps and sharing

When you call a pharmacy, your phone's dialer opens; when you show it on a map, a map
application (Google Maps) opens with the pharmacy's name and address. When you share results,
the system share sheet opens and you decide where they go. Those apps and services are governed
by their own privacy policies.

## 8. In-app purchases

If paid features are added to the App in the future, payments will be processed by the **Apple
App Store** or **Google Play**. Your payment and card details are never shared with the App or
the Developer. See [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) ·
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 9. Data we do not collect

The Developer does **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts,
health information, device identifiers or advertising identifier. There are no ads, third-party
analytics or tracking tools.

## 10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

## 11. Your rights

Since the App sends no personal data to the Developer, the Developer holds no records about you.
For any questions under applicable data protection laws (including Turkey's KVKK and the GDPR),
contact us at the address below.

## 12. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page; significant
changes are noted in the App's release notes.
